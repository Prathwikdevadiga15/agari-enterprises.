Our story

Three decades of trusted service

Agari Enterprises, led by Agari Raghavendra Rao, was established in 1992 in Yedapadavu. The business began by offering electricity payment services, cable network payment services, and retail sales of furniture and electronics.

Over the years, the company has grown steadily by focusing on excellent service and customer satisfaction. Today, Agari Enterprises is a leading electronics and furniture retail store in D.K., known for quality products, competitive prices, and trusted service. We continue to serve our loyal customers with care and commitment.
